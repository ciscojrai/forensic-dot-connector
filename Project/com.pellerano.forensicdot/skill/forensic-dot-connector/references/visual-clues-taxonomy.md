# Visual Clues Taxonomy

This taxonomy categorizes the visual evidence observable across century-spanning family archives, establishing standardized feature vectors for photographic forensic mining, heirloom identification, and genealogical reconstruction.

## 1. Physical Print Medium & Laboratory Artifacts

* **Edge Profiles & Margins**:
  * Scalloped / deckled edges: Decorative die-cut borders indicating commercial laboratory finishing (1938–1956).
  * White border margins with printed lab dates: Rubber ink stamp or photo-chemically exposed month/year (e.g., "JUN 61", "MAY 74").
  * Full-bleed borderless color prints: Common from the mid-1970s onward.
* **Instant Film Chemistry & Formats**:
  * Square image with asymmetric bottom chemical trap pod (Polaroid SX-70, 600, Time-Zero).
  * Peel-apart pack film remnants and roller-spread chemical unevenness.
* **In-Frame Embedded Timestamps**:
  * Orange / red 7-segment digital LED timestamp located in bottom-right corner (35mm Quartz Date Backs, late 1980s through 2000s).

## 2. Biographical Body Alterations, Medical Milestones & Grooming

* **Permanent Body Alterations & Tattoos**:
  * Tattoos (placement, ink style, sleeve progression): When user-verified (e.g., confirming a military enlistment or commemorative year) or displaying an embedded date, serves as a persistent lower-bound anchor ($T_{min} \ge \text{Acquisition Date}$) across subsequent photos of that individual. Without a confirmed acquisition date, tattoos serve as visual identity and longitudinal continuity anchors.
  * Piercings: Nostril, eyebrow, and cartilage piercings (surged in mainstream prominence late 1990s–2000s).
* **Temporary Physical & Medical Conditions (Micro-Timeline Windows)**:
  * Orthopedic casts (plaster vs colored fiberglass) and crutches: Establishes a highly constrained temporal micro-window (typically 4–10 weeks of healing time).
  * Dental braces and orthodontic headgear: Constrains youth subjects to an identifiable developmental window (typically ages 11–15).
* **Facial Hair & Grooming Eras**:
  * Distinctive facial hair phases (e.g., Dad's chevron/horseshoe mustache in the 1970s–1980s vs clean-shaven 1990s vs full beard revival).
  * Hair dye milestones, receding hairline progression, and prominent generational hairstyles (bouffant, perm, mullet, undercut).

## 3. Milestones, Domestic Pets & Life Objects

* **Sibling Milestones & Life Objects**:
  * Children's bicycles, tricycles, training wheels, and skateboards: Enables automated chronological sequencing to resolve milestone disputes (e.g., "Who got a bicycle first, Sam or Pat?"). Note: Names reflect user-assigned labels on facial clusters; age references (e.g., ~age 7) represent developmental visual estimates calibrated against user-supplied birth years.
  * First vehicle purchases, prom corsages, graduation caps, school uniforms.
* **Domestic Pets & Animal Companions**:
  * Household pets (cats, dogs), breeds, distinctive coat markings, and growth stages (kitten/puppy vs mature adult companion).
  * Pinpoints family milestone inquiries (e.g., "What year did Mom adopt our first cat?").

## 4. Personal Attire, Jewelry & Generational Fashion

* **Generational Fashion Eras**:
  * **1900s–1920s**: High-collared Victorian lace, corseted silhouettes, three-piece wool suits with pocket watches.
  * **1930s–1950s**: Tailored A-line dresses, padded shoulders; 1950s poodle skirts, saddle shoes, letterman jackets.
  * **1960s–1970s**: Mod mini-skirts, tie-dye patterns, bell-bottom jeans, polyester leisure suits with wide butterfly collars.
  * **1980s–1990s**: Power-suit shoulder pads, acid-wash denim, neon spandex; 1990s grunge flannels, oversized windbreakers.
  * **2000s–Present**: Y2K low-rise denim, velour tracksuits, skinny jeans, modern minimalist athleisure.
* **Heirlooms, Chronological Anchors & Accessories**:
  * **Heirloom Anchors (Cross-Photo Chronological Vectors)**: One known family heirloom—such as Grandfather's 1958 Omega watch, Mother's 1972 engagement ring, or a 1968 university signet ring—propagates hard lower bounds ($T_{min}$) to every unlabeled candid photo in which it glints on a subject's hand or wrist.
  * Wedding bands (courtship vs marriage states: absence of ring vs gold band vs modern silicone).
  * Eyewear frames (browline horn-rims, oversized aviators, minimalist wire rectangles, thick acetate).
  * Watches (pocket watches, mechanical analog, digital LCDs, modern OLED smartwatches).

## 5. Technology, Domestic Interiors & Automotive Context

* **Home Entertainment & Computers**:
  * Console tube radios (1930s–1940s), wooden television cabinets (1950s–1970s), boomboxes, beige CRT monitors (1990s), ultra-thin OLED flatscreens (2010s+).
* **Automotive Silhouettes**:
  * External running boards, chrome tailfins (1955–1960), wood-paneled station wagons (1970s–1980s), boxy sedans, round sealed-beam headlamps, modern LED daytime running lights.

## 6. Large-Scale Archive Verification Standards

* Cross-indexes visual markers across 10,000+ photo collections and links recurring individuals into dynamic family tree graphs.
* Propagates known historical dates automatically across newly ingested batches (+1k to +4k photos).
